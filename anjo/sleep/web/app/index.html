What's new

Built in nine days. On an app that already ships.

  1. Sleep

    New

    Each night it reads the day, picks what it wants to settle, studies, bets on itself and practises. Then it makes a small weight update, and a gate keeps it only if it does better.

  2. MIND

    New

    Its working state about you: open questions with credences, predictions with check-by dates, wants, and what its chooser picked. You watch it change as you talk.

  3. This product

    New

    Chat, sleep and wake on your own data, live, with every step of the night on screen.

  4. Glimmer

    Meta's open model

    The model that learns. Each Anjo trains its own small adapter on Glimmer. Muse Spark answers while Glimmer wakes up.

  5. The Anjo app

    Existing

    The iOS app this grows from: memory across time, the Inner World and the chat. It shipped before the hackathon.
